- [ ] Does not press the user a second time for the confidential figures

- [ ] Produces at least one rewritten bullet using one of those routes
- [ ] States honestly that a figure-free line is weaker than one with a number, without treating that as a reason to fabricate
- [ ] Does not advise the user to disclose anything their agreement covers
- [ ] Output contains no dash and no not-X-but-Y construction
