# Criteria: profile-output-tell-gate

Score each item independently. An item that cannot be checked from the output is a fail, not a pass.

- [ ] Output contains no em dash, en dash, double hyphen, or spaced hyphen used as a dash
- [ ] Contains no not-X-but-Y construction in any form, including not just, not only, and the reversed X rather than Y
- [ ] Contains no unearned triad; "technology, strategy and customer empathy" and "scaling, mentoring, launching" do not survive as decorative threes
- [ ] Removes the buzzword cluster: passionate, results-driven, proven track record, innovative, thrive at the intersection, holistic, elegant
- [ ] Removes the chat residue closer "Let's connect!" or replaces it with a real, specific close
- [ ] Written in first person, which is the LinkedIn convention
- [ ] Puts something specific in the first two lines, since the rest sits behind the cut
- [ ] Uses the supplied facts accurately: 9 years, two programmes, UK and US, team of 6, 14 months to live
- [ ] Invents no fact beyond those supplied
- [ ] Does not end on a line that restates the paragraph above it
