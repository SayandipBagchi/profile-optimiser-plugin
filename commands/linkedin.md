Use the `profile-optimiser` skill in rewrite mode, on the LinkedIn surface. Read the LinkedIn section of the surface guide first.

LinkedIn is the one surface where voice is an asset. Write the About section in first person, put the point in the first two lines because the rest is hidden behind the cut, and keep the writer's opinions, bluntness and specifics instead of flattening them into a professional register.

For the headline, return three options. Each says what they do, who for, and one proof point or specialism. A headline that is only the job title is not one of the three.

Strip the buzzword cluster (passionate, driven, results-driven, thought leader, dynamic, proven track record), the third-person bio voice, emoji separators, and anything that would be equally true of every other person with the same title.

Keep titles, dates and company names identical to the resume. Never write a recommendation or endorsement as though another named person wrote it.
